CS-1201 Object-Oriented Programming

Instructor: Arbish Akram

This course covers the principles and practice of object-oriented programming using C++. Building on procedural programming, students learn to design programs using classes, objects, inheritance, polymorphism, and templates. Emphasis is placed on clean design, reusability, and real-world problem solving.

Prerequisites

Programming Fundamentals.

Project

Students will complete a semester-long project in groups of five, applying OOP principles to a problem of their choosing. Proposal due Week 5; final submission and demo during the last week of the semester.
