Arbish Akram

Lecturer, Computer Science · GCU Lahore

I completed my PhD in the Department of Computer Science at the University of the Punjab (PUCIT), advised by Dr. Nazar Khan. I am currently serving as a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science at Government College University (GCU), Lahore. My research interests are in computer vision, machine learning, deep learning and image processing.
